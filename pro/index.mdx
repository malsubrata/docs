**TeraWallet Pro** is a premium add-on for the free [TeraWallet](/getting-started/key-features) core
plugin. It adds the money-out and lifecycle features that turn a store wallet into a full digital
economy: customer withdrawals, wallet-only coupons, automatic credit expiry, bulk balance imports,
and AffiliateWP payouts.

<Note>
  Pro **requires the free TeraWallet core plugin** to be installed and active — it extends core and
  cannot run on its own. See [Free vs Pro](/getting-started/pro) for a side-by-side comparison.
</Note>

## What you unlock

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Wallet Withdrawal" icon="money-bill-transfer" href="/pro/withdrawal">
    Let customers cash out their balance to PayPal, Stripe, Razorpay, Paystack, Cashfree or bank
    transfer, with admin approval and per-gateway fees.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Wallet Coupons" icon="ticket" href="/pro/wallet-coupons">
    Create coupons that credit the wallet instead of discounting the cart, with email/domain limits,
    usage caps and expiry.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Credit Expiry (FIFO)" icon="hourglass-half" href="/pro/wallet-expiry">
    Expire store credit on a schedule, spend the soonest-to-expire credit first, and send reminder
    and lapse emails.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bulk Importer" icon="file-csv" href="/pro/importer">
    Import thousands of credit/debit transactions from a CSV with batch processing and error logs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AffiliateWP Integration" icon="handshake" href="/pro/affiliatewp">
    Pay affiliate commissions straight into the wallet, crediting on paid referrals and reversing on
    refunds.
  </Card>
  <Card title="License & Updates" icon="key" href="/pro/installation">
    Activate your license for automatic updates and support.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Requirements

| Requirement | Version |
| --- | --- |
| TeraWallet (core) | Required, latest recommended |
| WordPress | 6.0 or higher |
| WooCommerce | 7.0 or higher |
| PHP | 7.4 or higher (8.2+ recommended) |
| AffiliateWP | Optional — only for affiliate payouts |
